## 5. Disclosures the application must not hide

Underwriters will ask. Answer yes/no from facts — do not soften.

1. **Pointed metal tip.** Radiused / blunt, not a knife, but it is designed to puncture metal cans.
2. **Pressurized cans.** Standard 12 oz aluminum beer / seltzer cans hold pressure. Hole = spray, sharp metal, possible eye/hand injury.
3. **Alcohol-adjacent intended use.** Shotgunning is the reason the punch exists. This is **not** liquor liability (we are not serving or selling alcohol on this SKU). It **is** a novelty tool used to speed alcohol intake.
4. **Rapid consumption risk.** Intoxication spike, choking, cut lips/hands, secondary accidents. Category peers still carry product-liability cover and publish 21+ / own-risk pages.
5. **Age.** 21+ only. Not a toy. Not a frat-hazing aid.
6. **Contract manufacturing.** US CNC shops make the part; SIDESHOT specifies, brands, and sells.
7. **No claims history** on this SKU (new product). Founder states any other commercial claims on the entity.
8. **Warning card** in every unit (draft pending counsel).
9. **Site 21+ copy** on every public page ([SMI-148](https://linear.app/smileing-goats/issue/SMI-148/safeshot-product-page-pre-order-path)).

### Misuse exclusion (read this)

ERGO NEXT’s public product-liability FAQ excludes injuries from **use the manufacturer did not intend**. If the application says “bottle opener only” and a claim is a shotgunning cut or alcohol injury, the carrier can call it misuse.

**Application wording to use:**

> Intended use includes puncturing the sidewall of a 12 oz aluminum beverage can so an adult 21+ can drink from the hole, and opening crown-cap bottles and stay-on tabs. We sell only with a 21+ warning card. We do not intend use as a weapon, on glass as a punch, or by anyone under 21.

If a carrier will not accept that sentence, **do not bind that quote**.

## 6. Limits (founder picks; no price attached)

Ask each quote for **$1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate** products-completed operations as the starting specimen. Higher if the carrier will write it. We do not invent what that costs.

Also ask (no need to buy on day one if they unbundle):

- Personal and advertising injury (site / 21+ claims)
- Damage to rented premises (events later)
- Additional insured / vendor wording if Amazon or a shop requires it
- Product-recall expense is **usually excluded** from GL — note the gap; do not invent a recall premium
